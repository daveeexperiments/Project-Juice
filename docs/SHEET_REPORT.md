## Reviewing a guess

Open the manifest in `art/manifest/`, pick a piece, and set `kind` to what it
should collide as. Then tick `reviewed`. The slicer never overwrites either
field, so a re-run costs you nothing.

| kind | collides as | for |
|---|---|---|
| `POLYGON` | the traced outline, closed by the ball radius | terrain you stand on |
| `CIRCLE` | one radius | gears, pulleys, wheels, saw blades |
| `RECT` | the region's box | beams, straight pipes, platforms |
| `ANNULUS` | two radii, inner surface solid | a ring the ball travels inside |
| `NONE` | nothing at all | decoration, in every placement |
